FAQ

Common questions

How much film does my car need?

One roll covers 22.8 m². That is usually enough for a full sedan. Larger cars and SUVs may need more. Send us your car model on WhatsApp and we will tell you exactly how many rolls to order.

What is the difference between the two clear films?

Car Protection Roll is a clear film that keeps your original paint color visible. Anti-Dust Ultra Strong is also clear, with a stronger build made to resist dust and daily wear.

How much does installation cost?

Installation is 1,200 JD per roll, on top of the 500 JD roll price. Tick "Add professional installation" when you order and we will arrange a time with you.

How fast is delivery, and does it cost anything?

Delivery is free and instant. We confirm your order by phone and send the roll right away.

Can I buy the roll and install it myself?

Yes. Installation is optional. The roll price stays 500 JD whether or not you choose installation.
